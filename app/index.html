Separate audio tracks or compressed audio?

MXF and MOV files with several audio tracks (eight mono tracks, say) are combined in track order; pick the layout above. For compressed audio (AAC, AC-3) in an MP4 or MOV, pull out a WAV first:

ffmpeg -i master.mp4 -map 0:a:0 -c:a pcm_s24le mix.wav

Example report

Drop the report your QC platform produced. Gatecheck reads its loudness results and works out the gain that passes every test, using the platform's own measurements. Reports are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Drop QC reports here SDVI Qualify reports and HardingFPA certificates are read exactly. Any other report (PDF, XML, CSV or text) goes through the general reader: you confirm the values it finds.

    Match a QC report

    Type the integrated loudness a QC platform reported for this file. Gatecheck finds which measurement method reproduces it, including common channel-mapping mistakes.

      Calibration

      Offsets learned from QC reports you have matched. After a few files, Gatecheck predicts what each platform will report before you deliver. Saved in this browser.

      Correct to spec

      A static gain change to hit the selected spec's target with its own gate. True peak is checked after the gain.

      Report

      Every reading, every spec verdict and the QC match for this file, as a PDF for the client or a CSV for the ticket or the QC log.

      The dialogue detector is Gatecheck's own (speech-band energy, syllabic modulation and pauses). Commercial detectors differ, so dialogue-gated values can sit a few tenths of an LU away from a QC platform's. Use calibration to close that gap.
